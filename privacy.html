Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains what the Norme mobile app collects, why, who else sees it, and how to get it deleted. It describes what the app actually does — not a generic template.

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — email address, full name, username, and password (stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form). A phone number if you choose to add one.
  • Profile details — display name, bio, profile picture, location, and, for seller accounts, business name, category and business phone.
  • Content you create — posts (photos and videos with captions), wardrobe item photos, moodboards, comments, reactions, saved items, and direct messages you send to other users.
  • Photos you upload for your Norme Model — the selfies you submit so the app can generate your avatar.
  • Marketplace and event details — shipping address and order contents when you buy something, and ticket selections when you buy event tickets.

Information created as you use the app

  • Activity — which posts you view, save, share, and which brand pages you open. This drives your feed and your Weekly Recap.
  • Transaction records — amount, currency, what the payment was for, its status and reference.

If you sign in with Google

We receive your email address, name and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password.

2. Device permissions

  • Camera — to take photos and videos for posts, wardrobe items and your Norme Model.
  • Microphone — only while recording a video.
  • Photos / media library — to let you pick existing photos to post or add to your wardrobe.

The app reads photos only when you actively choose them or use the wardrobe import screen. It does not scan your library in the background.

3. Who else receives your data

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the services below, only to make a feature work.

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Google (Gemini API) The images you submit for your Norme Model (your selfies) and your wardrobe photos. Text you send to the styling assistant, and the titles/notes of your wardrobe items when you ask for outfit suggestions. To generate your avatar, detect and cut out garments, and produce styling suggestions. This processing happens on Google's servers, not on your device.
Paystack Your email address, the amount, currency and a payment reference. For M-Pesa, also the phone number you enter. To take payment. Card details are entered on Paystack's own secure checkout and never reach Norme's servers.
Our email provider Your email address. To send account verification and password reset emails.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile and content are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Photos you submit for your Norme Model are kept so you can regenerate or restore a previous version, and are deleted when you delete the avatar or your account.
  • Payment records are kept after account deletion, with your identity removed, because we are required to retain financial records. They can no longer be linked back to you.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. You will be asked for your password to confirm.

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your profile, username, email, phone and password
  • your posts, photos and videos
  • your wardrobe items and their photos
  • your Norme Model, including the selfies you uploaded and every generated version
  • your moodboards, saves, reactions, follows and messages
  • your activity history and Weekly Recaps

Payment records are retained as described above, with your identity removed. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, email us at the address below from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

6. Your choices

  • You can edit or remove your profile details and delete individual posts and wardrobe items at any time.
  • You can make your account private so only approved followers see your content.
  • You can revoke camera, microphone or photo permissions in your device settings; features that need them will stop working.
  • You can request a copy of your data by emailing us.

7. Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to production data is limited to the people who operate the service.

8. Children

Norme is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be announced in the app.
