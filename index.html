WHAT'S INSIDE

One app, the whole culture.

Culture

Editorial feeds built around real people — Designers, Models, Brands, OOTD, Rising Stars, and the collaborations between them. Discover who's shaping the culture, not just what's trending.

Shop

A real marketplace for real pieces — list, browse, save, and check out securely with Card or M-Pesa. Every listing comes from an actual seller in the community, nothing staged.

Stylist

Import your own wardrobe and let AI put outfits together for you — real suggestions pulled from pieces you actually own, not a generic lookbook.

Moodboards

Scrapbook the way you actually think about style — photos, quotes, songs, and places pinned together into a board that's yours, shareable whenever you want.

Portfolios

Real Designer and Model profiles — finished designs, sketches from the process, full collections, and the brands they work with. A portfolio, not just a bio.

Events

Fashion weeks, runway shows, and pop-ups — browse, pick your ticket tier, pay with Card or M-Pesa, and walk in with a QR code, not a screenshot of a DM.

FOR THE PEOPLE MAKING IT

Built for creators, not just consumers.

Whether you're a designer building a portfolio, a model building a following, a stylist building a wardrobe, or someone who just loves getting dressed — Norme gives you a real place to do it, not another feed to disappear into.
